# Label-free optical imaging of macrophage/monocyte activation states and public availability of the image data

Scope note: "label-free" here = brightfield/phase contrast, quantitative phase imaging (QPI), digital holographic microscopy (DHM), optical diffraction tomography (ODT)/holotomography, ptychography, Raman/SRS, light scattering, and autofluorescence (FLIM/hyperspectral) methods that need no exogenous label. Cell Painting is **not** label-free (6 fluorescent dyes) but is included under "morphology-based profiling" because the assignment asks for it; it is flagged as stained throughout.

Headline result up front: across every study I could read in full, **I found zero deposits of raw label-free image data for macrophage/monocyte polarization**. The only public deposit found is a ~24 KB table of derived optical-scattering features (Dryad). Every other paper says "available from the corresponding author on reasonable request", "available in [internal system], access by email", or deposits only extracted feature tables/source-data spreadsheets.

## Q1. Which published studies classify macrophage polarization from label-free morphology with (deep) learning, and did they release their images?

### Takeaway
A handful of studies classify M0/M1/M2 from label-free single-cell signatures (morphology+Raman, light scattering, AFM mechanics, FLIM, hyperspectral reflectance), with accuracies ~72–98%, but none used deep learning on brightfield/phase images alone, and none deposited raw images; the only public artifact is a derived-feature table on Dryad.

### Cited Findings
- **Pavillon, Hobro, Akira & Smith, PNAS 2018, 115(12)** "Noninvasive detection of macrophage activation with single-cell resolution through machine learning" — Osaka University, Immunology Frontier Research Center (IFReC) / Dept. of Applied Physics. Combined quantitative phase imaging (morphology) + single-cell Raman with machine learning to detect LPS activation. PMID 29511099, PMC5866539 — [Europe PMC record](https://europepmc.org/article/MED/29511099). I was blocked (HTTP 403 / reCAPTCHA) from both pnas.org and PMC, so **I could not read its Methods or data-availability statement**; treat any numeric detail for this specific paper as unverified. A web-search summary attributed "LPS at 1,000 ng/mL for 24 h" and "models were generated based on the known state of stimulation (control or exposed to LPS)" to it — [search-surfaced summary of the PNAS paper](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.1711872115) — not independently verified by me.
- **Pavillon & Smith, Scientific Reports 2019, 9:17054** "Immune cell type, cell activation, and single cell heterogeneity revealed by label-free optical methods" (PMC6864054, Osaka IFReC; corresponding authors n-pavillon@ifrec.osaka-u.ac.jp, nsmith@ap.eng.osaka-u.ac.jp). Pairs **morphological indicators from label-free imaging with single-cell Raman** across **3 cell types × 2 stimulation conditions (±LPS)**; cell types include **RAW (RAW264.7) cells and primary peritoneal macrophages**. Raman indicators alone "can distinguish 6 cases, composed of 3 cell types and 2 stimulation conditions"; pairing morphology + molecular indicators "yields a significant improvement". PCA: "morphology and Raman variables … account for 40–50% of the data variance"; "Raw cells are strongly separated from peritoneal macrophages both in terms of morphological and molecular phenotype." **Data availability, verbatim: "The raw data and datasets acquired and generated during the current study are available from the corresponding author on reasonable request."** — [PMC6864054](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6864054/)
- **Dannhauser, Rossi, Di Giuseppe, Netti, Terrazzano, Causa et al., Royal Society Open Science 2022, 9:220270** "Single cell classification of macrophage subtypes by label-free cell signatures and machine learning" (PMID 36177192, PMC9515641; University of Naples Federico II + Italian Institute of Technology). **Wide-angle static light scattering** (ad hoc microfluidic apparatus), human monocyte-derived macrophages from the **blood bank of Federico II medical school; 3 healthy donors**; cell counts **monocytes n=148 (107 in one comparison), M0 n=149, M1 n=167, M2 n=215**, ">600 cells from three different donors". Four extracted biophysical properties (diameter D, N/C ratio, nuclear RI, cytoplasmic RI; cytoplasmic RI ≈1.39 for M0/M2, ≈1.38 M1, ≈1.36 monocytes). Accuracy: **M1 vs M2 = 85.1%**, **M0 vs M1 vs M2 = 72.3%**, monocytes vs macrophages **≥99.2%** (linear/quadratic/cubic SVM). **Data accessibility, verbatim: "Datasets for this research work are available from the Dryad Digital Repository: doi:10.5061/dryad.1ns1rn8wh"** — [PMC9515641](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9515641/)
  - Dryad record: **"Optical signature dataset for living macrophages and monocytes"**, David Dannhauser (Univ. Naples Federico II) et al., **license CC0-1.0**, **storage size 23,964 bytes (~24 KB)**, 1 version — [Dryad API record](https://datadryad.org/api/v2/datasets/doi%3A10.5061%2Fdryad.1ns1rn8wh). **Skeptical read: at ~24 KB this is a numeric feature/signature table, not raw scattering images or micrographs.**
- **Hourani, Perez-Gonzalez, Khoshmanesh, Luwor, Achuthan, Baratchi, O'Brien-Simpson & Al-Hourani, Scientific Reports 2023, 13:5202** "Label-free macrophage phenotype classification using machine learning methods" (PMC10061362; RMIT University School of Engineering + Univ. Melbourne/Royal Melbourne Hospital, Melbourne Cytometry Platform, Fiona Elsey Cancer Research Institute, Melbourne Dental School). **Murine RAW 264.7**; 0.25×10⁶ cells/mL in 6-well plates; **24 h** stimulation: M1 = IFN-γ 25 ng/mL then LPS 25 ng/mL; M2a = IL-4 25 ng/mL; M2b = immune complexes (14 µg OVA + anti-OVA) + LPS 50 ng/mL; M2c = IL-10 50 ng/mL + TGF-β 50 ng/mL; M2d = LPS 50 ng/mL + NECA 50 µM; brefeldin A last 5 h for intracellular stains. The "label-free" signal is **autofluorescence/light-scatter across 45 flow-cytometry parameters** (Beckman Coulter CytoFLEX LX, 22 APD detectors, gain 500), **152,438 live single-cell events**, split 60/20/20. Classifiers KNN (k=50), SVM (ECOC multiclass), fully connected NN (1 hidden layer, 100 neurons; 45→6). **Accuracy: FCNN 75.8% on the 6-class test set** (76.7% train); pairwise 92.0%, triplets 91.9%, quadruplets 84.2% (Results text says 84.1%), quintuplets 80.4%. Imaging in the paper is **widefield immunofluorescence (FLoid Cell Imaging Station; PKH67 membrane + DAPI; FITC ex482/18 em532/59, DAPI ex390/40 em446/33)** — i.e. stained, with no objective/NA/pixel size reported. Paired readouts: flow cytometry (CD86, iNOS, CD206, CD204, TLR1, CD69, IL-12/IL-23p40, IL-10 secretion, VEGF), Griess NO assay, arginase/urea assay; **no qPCR, RNA-seq or proteomics**. **Data availability, verbatim: "All data will be available on reasonable request from the corresponding author."** No accession/DOI/URL. — [PMC10061362](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10061362/)
- **Chen, Wu, … Himly, Italiani, Boraschi, Zhang, Galluzzi, Li; Small Methods 2026** "AFM-Based Deep Learning Decodes Human Macrophage Mechanophenotypes" (PMID 40722235; Laboratory of Inflammation and Vaccines, Shenzhen Institutes of Advanced Technology, CAS). Label-free **AFM nanoscale force mapping** (Young's modulus, adhesion, sphericity) + **deep neural network** with "pixel-wise data enhancement and a meta-confidence estimator"; "accurately distinguishes naïve (M0), M1, and M2 functional phenotypes of human macrophages, even across donor heterogeneity". No accuracy figure in the abstract; no data-availability statement visible in the Europe PMC record (full text not open). — [Europe PMC/abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40722235/)
- **Neto, O'Rourke, Zhang, Fitzgerald, Dunne & Monaghan; eLife 2022;11:e77373** "Non-invasive classification of macrophage polarisation by 2P-FLIM and machine learning" (PMC9578711; Trinity College Dublin, Dept. of Mechanical, Manufacturing and Biomedical Engineering). **Label-free two-photon NAD(P)H/FAD autofluorescence FLIM**; 24 h-polarised human macrophages in ibidi Luer µ-slides (0.8 mm channel); custom upright **Olympus BX61WI**, 80 MHz Ti:sapphire **Chameleon Ultra**, **25× water-immersion objective, 1.05 NA**, 37°C stage; excitation **760 nm (NAD(P)H)** and **800 nm (FAD)**; FCCP perturbation during imaging; **random forests** classifier on full-field-of-view and single-cell FLIM variables, evaluated by **ROC/AUC** (authors state "there is no extra data available for testing our trained model", so AUC is on training/OOB data — a real methodological caveat); single-cell performance "impacted by donor variability"; **6 donors** referenced in reviewer correspondence. **Data availability, verbatim: "All data generated or analysed during this study are included in the manuscript and supporting files (uploaded as source data)."** i.e. **source-data spreadsheets of FLIM variables/UMAP/ML outputs, not raw FLIM images** (e.g. "Figure 3—source data 1. Fluorescence lifetime imaging microscopy (FLIM) imaging and corresponding variables measurement for each replicate, Uniform Manifold Approximate and Projection (UMAP) analysis, and machine l[earning]…"). — [PMC9578711](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9578711/)
- **Hyperspectral reflectance confocal, Scientific Reports 2017, 8121** "Classification of M1/M2-polarized human macrophages by label-free hyperspectral reflectance confocal microscopy and multivariate analysis" (PMC5566322; acknowledges IFT-CNR Rome collaborators). Human **MDMs**; label-free hyperspectral reflectance lambda stacks; **PCA + linear discriminant analysis**; "single-cell-level in vitro classification of M1 vs. M2 macrophages in a non-invasive and label-free manner with a high accuracy (**above 98% for cells deriving from the same donor**)"; paired flow/IF markers (CD86 high in M1; CD163 "was not successful in discriminating the two groups with statistical significance"). **No "Data availability" section exists in this paper** (I searched the full text: "Data availab" → NOT FOUND); the only shared imaging is **Supplementary Videos SV1/SV2 (pseudo-coloured lambda stacks for M1 and M2 MDMs)** and Supplementary Figure S1 spectra. — [PMC5566322](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5566322/)
- **In vivo human dermis 2P-FLIM, eLife 2022;11:e72819** "Label-free imaging of M1 and M2 macrophage phenotypes in the human dermis in vivo using two-photon excited FLIM": in vitro monocyte-derived M2 macrophages showed longer mean NAD(P)H lifetimes (~1,185 ps) than M1 (~479 ps); a decision-tree model separates M1/M2 from other skin cells. — [eLife 72819](https://elifesciences.org/articles/72819). I did not read its data-availability section.
- **Mezhenskyi et al., Advanced Intelligent Discovery 2025/2026** "Macrophage Phenotype Detection Methodology on Textured Surfaces via Nuclear Morphology Using Machine Learning" — [Wiley](https://advanced.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/aidi.202500055). **Wiley returned HTTP 403**; I could not verify whether the nuclear-morphology input is label-free (nuclear morphology usually implies a DNA stain), nor the model, accuracy or data-availability statement. Flagged as unverified.

### Inferences
- The field's label-free "classification of polarization" literature is dominated by **spectroscopic/mechanical/scattering feature vectors + classical ML (SVM, random forests, LDA, shallow NN)**, not by CNNs on raw brightfield/phase images. A deep-learning-on-label-free-images study of macrophage polarization, with released images, appears **not to exist in the indexed literature as of Oct 2026**.
- Reported accuracies drop sharply when donor heterogeneity and >2 classes are included (98% same-donor HSI; 85% M1-vs-M2 and 72% three-class light scattering; 75.8% six-class autofluorescence), which is the single most reusable quantitative pattern for a report.
- Because no raw images are public, any new effort on label-free macrophage-state classification would have to generate its own imaging dataset; this is a genuine open niche (and a reason a deposited dataset would have outsized value).

### Gaps
- PNAS 2018 (Pavillon et al.) Methods/data-availability could not be read (publisher 403 + PMC bot wall). Its companion 2019 Sci Rep paper from the same lab says data are available "on reasonable request", so a deposit for the 2018 paper is unlikely, but I did not confirm it.
- No accuracy value, settings, or DAS obtained for the AFM+DNN Small Methods 2026 paper (paywalled).
- Mezhenskyi (Adv Intell Discovery) unverifiable (403).
- I found **no study at all** that images **trained (β-glucan/BCG-trained) vs tolerant (LPS-tolerized)** macrophages with QPI/DHM/holotomography. The closest is a Chinese-language methods paper: "[A DC-iDEP-based fast and high-resolution method for detection of LPS tolerance of RAW264.7 macrophages and screening of therapeutic agents]", *Sheng Wu Gong Cheng Xue Bao* 2024, PMID 39584340 — label-free **dielectrophoresis**, not imaging; I did not read its full text. — [Europe PMC search](https://europepmc.org/search?query=EXT_ID:39584340)

## Q2. QPI / DHM / holographic tomography of macrophage activation: which groups, and is the data public?

### Takeaway
There is a small, mostly 2024–2026 and mostly Asian-lab QPI/DHM/ODT literature on macrophage activation that is descriptive (volume, dry mass, projected area, motility) rather than classificatory, and none of it deposits images; I found **no** macrophage-polarization QPI paper from the Popescu (Phi Optics/SLIM), Park–Yaqoob–Dasari (Tomocube/MIT), Marquet, Kemper or Tolde/Rosel/Brabek groups.

### Cited Findings
- **Bai, Lu, Zhu, Li et al., Journal of Biomedical Optics 2026, 31(6):066002** "Macrophage volume analysis and real-time monitoring by digital holography" (PMID 42245197, PMC13233025; **Shanghai University of Medicine & Health Sciences**, with Univ. of Shanghai for Science and Technology; first author trained at Nanjing Univ. of Science and Technology and MGH; corresponding: baibd@sumhs.edu.cn, zhug@sumhs.edu.cn). **RAW 264.7** in RPMI-1640 + 10% FBS; **M0** = adherent cells after 2–3 h; **M1 = 200 ng/mL LPS, 24 h**; **M2 = 20 ng/mL IL-4, 24 h**; volume from phase assuming **n_cell = 1.38, n_medium = 1.33** ("effective optical volume"). **Time-lapse M0→M1 for ≥8 h, hourly sampling** ("the volume evolution curve was plotted based on the maximum volume recorded in each hour"; "four representative volume values at each time point"), with four distinct phases: slow rise 0–2 h ("mild activation phase dominated by metabolic upregulation and early cytoskeletal reorganization"), **steep increase 6–8 h** ("a strong activation burst, likely associated with actin polymerization, pseudopod formation, and enhanced inflammatory functionality"); dying cells show progressive volume decrease. Paired readout: **fluorescent phalloidin F-actin after 4% PFA/0.5% Triton X-100** (endpoint only). **No "Data Availability" statement exists in the paper** — searching the full text for "availab" returns only the author-biography sentence ("Biographies of the other authors are not available") and the funding statement (NSFC 62105197; SUMHS AMSCP-23-04-01; Shanghai Education Commission AI programme A1-2601-24-201008-5). No cytokine/qPCR/flow validation of M1 identity. — [PMC13233025](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13233025/) / [SPIE](https://www.spiedigitallibrary.org/journals/journal-of-biomedical-optics/volume-31/issue-06/066002/Macrophage-volume-analysis-and-real-time-monitoring-by-digital-holography/10.1117/1.JBO.31.6.066002.full)
- **Sun, Zhu, Yang, Liu, Shi & Peng; J Vis Exp (JoVE) 2026** "Real-Time Label-Free Imaging and Quantitative Analysis of Macrophage Morphodynamics Using Optical Diffraction Tomography" (PMID 42372054; Sichuan Provincial Key Laboratory of Innovation and Efficient Utilization of Chinese Medicine Germplasm Resources, **Chengdu University of Traditional Chinese Medicine**). **RAW264.7**, **LPS stimulation with baicalin pretreatment**, long-term time-lapse ODT; extracted parameters = **projected area, perimeter, average migration speed**; "representative cells were selected for tracking and quantitative analysis". Protocol paper; **no instrument model, NA, voxel size, frame interval or data-availability statement visible in the record**, and JoVE pages returned empty to fetch. — [Europe PMC abstract](https://europepmc.org/article/MED/42372054); [JoVE DOI](https://doi.org/10.3791/71209)
- **Saha, Alizada, Zangle & Ghandehari; Nanomedicine (Lond) 2026** "Quantitative phase imaging analysis of silica nanoparticle-induced macrophage recovery and functionality" (PMID 42483852; **Utah Center for Nanomedicine, University of Utah** — Thomas Zangle's QPI lab). Real-time QPI of **cellular mass, specific growth rate, population distribution and viability** in macrophages exposed to silica nanoparticles of differing size/porosity, plus QPI+fluorescence for **bioparticle phagocytosis at single-cell level**; "dose-dependent macrophage recovery upon SNP-treatment, and the phagocytic assay showed no decrease in phagocytosis at nontoxic SNP concentrations". This is **nanoparticle toxicity/phagocytic function, not M1/M2 polarization**. No DAS accessible (paywalled). — [Europe PMC abstract](https://europepmc.org/article/MED/42483852)
- Holotomography vendor landscape (for interpreting user papers): "In 2012 the first commercial HT company Nanolive was founded, later followed by Tomocube in 2014"; a review's figure panels show "a mouse macrophage and an LPS-treated mouse macrophage" with the **images credited to Tomocube** — i.e. an illustrative vendor figure, not a dedicated macrophage study. — [Holotomography review/Wikipedia summary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holotomography)
- Nanolive macrophage protocol (label-free, but lipid droplets rather than polarization): **"Visualization of Lipid Droplets in the Alveolar Macrophage Cell Line MH-S with Live-cell Imaging by 3D Holotomographic Microscopy (Nanolive)", Bio-protocol 2023** (PMC9993077). — [PMC9993077](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9993077/)
- Adjacent label-free ODT/holotomography immune-cell papers that came up but are **not** macrophage polarization: "Quantitative Optical Diffraction Tomography Imaging of Mouse Platelets" (Front Physiol 2020, PMC7526686); "Unveiling Lipid Droplet Transport Dynamics as Biomarkers of Senescence Using Label-Free, Time-Lapse Holotomography" (Aging Dis 2025, PMC12727127); "Morphodynamic cellular changes prior to and during cell fusion related to osteoclast formation" (Biochem Biophys Rep 2026, PMC13470564); "CellSNAP: a fast, accurate algorithm for 3D cell segmentation in quantitative phase imaging" (J Biomed Opt 2024, PMC11025678); "Activation of lands cycle-mediated inflammation in living macrophages exposed to label-free particulate matter" (J Hazard Mater 2025, PMID 41046674). — [Europe PMC search listing](https://europepmc.org/search?query=macrophage%20AND%20%22optical%20diffraction%20tomography%22)
- Systematic search result: a Europe PMC query for `(macrophage*) AND ("quantitative phase imaging" OR "digital holographic" OR holotomography OR "optical diffraction tomography" OR "quantitative phase microscopy")) AND (polariz* OR activat* OR LPS OR "IL-4")` returned **304 hits**, of which the only on-target macrophage-activation QPI/DHM/ODT primary papers are the JBO 2026, JoVE 2026 and Nanomedicine 2026 entries above; a query for `(macrophage* OR monocyte*) AND (ptychograph* OR Phasefocus OR Livecyte OR "spatial light interference" OR "gradient light interference")` returned **no relevant primary study** (hits were unrelated hematology/oncology papers matching "SLIM"/"phase" as substrings). — [Europe PMC REST search](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/search?query=macrophage+AND+%22quantitative+phase+imaging%22&format=json)

### Inferences
- The named QPI groups in the assignment (Popescu/Phi Optics SLIM & GLIM, Park/Yaqoob/Dasari, Marquet, Kemper, Tolde/Rosel/Brabek) have published extensively on QPI but **apparently not on macrophage M1/M2 polarization**; macrophage QPI work instead comes from nanomedicine labs (Zangle/Ghandehari, Utah) and from Chinese optics/TCM groups (Shanghai, Chengdu).
- The JBO 2026 DHM paper is the closest thing to a published label-free time-course of M0→M1 with quantitative dynamics, but it is a single cell line, has no molecular validation of polarization, assumes a constant refractive index, and has no data statement at all — so it cannot be reused as a benchmark dataset.
- Ptychography (Phasefocus Livecyte) macrophage-polarization work appears absent from the peer-reviewed literature; if it exists, it is in vendor application notes only.

### Gaps
- I did not directly mine the Nanolive / Tomocube / Phasefocus / Phi Optics publication bibliographies (vendor sites were not fetched), so a macrophage-polarization user paper indexed only there could have been missed.
- No instrument/objective/NA/pixel-size/frame-interval details obtainable for the JoVE ODT protocol or the Utah QPI paper (both inaccessible in full text).

## Q3. Raman / stimulated Raman scattering of macrophage polarization — deposited data?

### Takeaway
Two independent Raman lineages exist (Popp/Leibniz-IPHT Jena on human MDM polarization; Pavillon–Smith Osaka on activation), both with single-cell classification, and **both state data are available only from the authors on request**; no Raman/SRS macrophage-polarization spectra or images are deposited anywhere I could find.

### Cited Findings
- **Naumann/Popp group (Leibniz-IPHT & Jena University Hospital): "Label-Free Characterization of Macrophage Polarization Using Raman Spectroscopy", Int J Mol Sci 2023, 24(1):824** (PMC9821063). Author initials N.A., M.N., U.N., C.K., O.W., R.R.G., I.R.; human cells under **Jena University Hospital ethics vote 5050-01/17 (3 March 2017)**, informed consent obtained; paired **flow cytometry** analysis ("N.A. and M.N. analyzed flow cytometry data"); Raman analysis by R.R.G. and N.A. A Raman-imaging observation reported in this literature: pro-inflammatory cells "appeared as a compact round shape with lipid droplets". **Data Availability Statement, verbatim: "Data is available from the authors upon request."** — [PMC9821063](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9821063/)
- **Pavillon & Smith (Osaka IFReC)** — Raman-based macrophage work with explicit "available from the corresponding author on reasonable request" DAS (Sci Rep 2019, PMC6864054, quoted verbatim in Q1). The same pair's related Raman immune-cell papers also report classification accuracies in the 84–87% range for activation states (as summarised in secondary search results). Additional Raman macrophage papers from this lab: "Label-free Raman mapping of saturated and unsaturated fatty acid uptake, storage, and return toward baseline levels in macrophages" (Analyst 2021, PMID 33346264); "Label-free Raman imaging of the macrophage response to the malaria pigment hemozoin" (Analyst 2015, PMID 25646175); "Deriving accurate molecular indicators of protein synthesis through Raman-based sparse classification" (Analyst 2021, PMID 33949431). — [Europe PMC author search](https://europepmc.org/search?query=AUTH%3A%22Pavillon%20N%22)
- SRS context (not polarization-specific but recent and label-free): "Physics-paired stimulated Raman scattering microscopy enables label-free phenotyping of lipid droplets 3D motility in live cells", *Light Sci Appl* 2026 (PMC13400741); "High-content stimulated Raman pathology imaging and transcriptomics reveal leukemia subtype-specific lipid metabolic heterogeneity", *Front Immunol* 2025 (PMC12520939); review "Raman spectroscopy combined with multiple technologies for label-free identification of immune cells: An overview", *J Pharm Anal* 2026 (PMC13312135). — [Europe PMC listing](https://europepmc.org/search?query=macrophage%20AND%20%22stimulated%20Raman%22)

### Inferences
- Raman spectra are small files (kilobytes per spectrum) and therefore trivially depositable; the fact that both leading groups still say "on request" indicates a norm/incentive gap rather than a technical one.
- Because polarization-labelled Raman spectra are not public, a report should treat published accuracies (84–87% Osaka; ~98% same-donor hyperspectral) as unreproducible from released artifacts.

### Gaps
- I did not locate any SRS/CARS study whose explicit design is M0/M1/M2 classification; the SRS hits concern lipid droplets or leukemia subtypes.
- The IJMS 2023 Jena paper's exact cell source (human MDM donor counts), stimulation doses and Raman settings (laser wavelength/power, step size, integration time) were not extracted — I only confirmed its ethics, flow-cytometry pairing and DAS.

## Q4. Morphology-based profiling of macrophage activation (shape descriptors, Cell Painting) — public image sets?

### Takeaway
Two substantive Cell Painting programmes profile macrophage polarization (Boehringer Ingelheim/Ulm on iPSC- and monocyte-derived macrophages; Örebro on THP-1/NLRP3), but **neither deposits raw images**: Boehringer keeps them in an internal system ("SImA"/"SImA3.1", access by email) and Örebro releases only treatment-level profiles, UMAP embeddings and code. Note both are fluorescent (6-dye PhenoVue), i.e. not label-free.

- Shape-descriptor evidence in the label-free literature: elongation/roundness is the canonical descriptor — the Boehringer papers use an explicit **cell-roundness Z-score** as their primary interpretable morphology metric (PMC11595184), and Raman-imaging work describes M1 cells as "a compact round shape with lipid droplets" (PMC9821063).

### Inferences
- The best-characterised macrophage polarization image collections that exist anywhere (Boehringer's IDM/MDM Cell Painting screens, four donors, 384-well replicates, Opera Phenix) are **industrially held and only email-accessible** — a strong candidate for a data request rather than a download.
- "Available in SImA / SImA3.1" is effectively a *not deposited* statement dressed as a deposit; a report should quote it as such.

### Gaps
- Whether Boehringer would actually grant image access on request is untested.
- JUMP-CP compound libraries were *used* in the Boehringer macrophage screen, but JUMP-CP's own imaging is in U2OS/A549 — so the macrophage images are new and unreleased.

## Q5. Which public image repositories actually hold macrophage/monocyte image sets at all?

### Takeaway
A systematic repository sweep finds macrophage images in BioImage Archive (36 studies, all fluorescence/EM/zebrafish infection, none label-free polarization), one classic BBBC benchmark of murine BMDMs (fluorescent), essentially nothing macrophage-specific in IDR or the Cell Painting Gallery, and only a ~24 KB derived-feature table in Dryad — i.e. **no public label-free macrophage/monocyte activation image set exists**.

### Cited Findings
- **EBI BioImage Archive / BioStudies — exact query `collection=BioImages&query=macrophage` returns totalHits = 36.** Notable hits (accession | content | release): **S-BIAD3375** "Longitudinal in vivo tracking of macrophage fate using fluorescence imaging" (Eric Lin, 2026-10-01); **S-BIAD4019** "Microscopy data: Swimming motility and type IV pili modulate phagocytosis by shaping physical interactions between Pseudomonas aeruginosa and macrophages" (Tania Distler, Alexandre Persat, EPFL, 2026-09-01); **S-BIAD4111** "LDHA-Dependent Redox Control of Selective mRNA Translation Directs Monocyte Fate Commitment" (Ning Yang, 2026-09-11); **S-BIAD1876** "GBP1 drives lysosomal activity against cytosol-invading mycobacteria in macrophages"; **S-BIAD1871** "Phagocytosis assay of murine alveolar macrophages with bacteriophage PAK_P1 and fluorescent beads"; **S-BIAD931** "ARL5b inhibits human rhinovirus 16 propagation and impairs macrophage-mediated bacterial clearance"; **S-BIAD1943** "S100a4+ alveolar macrophages accelerate the progression of precancerous atypical adenomatous hyperplasia…"; **S-BIAD2093/2094/2096** and **S-BIAD2361/2463** (Spaink/Meijer, Leiden — zebrafish macrophage infection); **S-BIAD2580** "A Host-Centric Morphological Profiling Approach to Identify Repurposed Antiviral Drugs" (Ringers, Rietdijk, Asp — Uppsala, 2026-05-26); **S-BIAD1116** "Deciphering functional tumor-immune crosstalk through integrated highly multiplexed imaging and deep visual proteomics"; **S-BIAD1184** whole-slide NOD mouse pancreas (CD11c/F4-80/IL-1β). **None is a label-free (brightfield/phase/QPI/Raman) macrophage activation-state dataset.** Per-study size/licence are not returned by the search API and would need per-accession lookup. — [BioStudies search API](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/biostudies/api/v1/search?query=macrophage&collection=BioImages&pageSize=100); portal: [BioImage Archive](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/bioimage-archive/)
- Related non-macrophage myeloid deposit: **S-BIAD564**, microglia efferocytosis / cytoskeletal dynamics. — [Euro-BioImaging news](https://www.eurobioimaging.eu/news/neuroscience-new-microglia-dataset-available-on-bioimage-archive/)
- **Image Data Resource (IDR) — I enumerated all 147 projects via the IDR API and keyword-filtered.** No macrophage/monocyte polarization study. Closest: **idr0054-segura-tonsilhyperion/experimentA**, "Human lymphoid organ cDC2 and macrophages play complementary roles in T follicular helper responses" — **imaging mass cytometry of tonsil sections** (not label-free microscopy, not in vitro polarization). The most relevant *methodological* precedent is **idr0109-zaritsky-melanoma/experimentA**, "Interpretable deep learning uncovers cellular properties in label-free live cell images that are predictive of highly metastatic melanoma" — "Live melanoma single cell phase contrast imaging with multiple labels: cell category (PDX, cell line, melanocyte, clonal expansion), PDX metastatic efficiency (high, low, unknown), cell type" — i.e. a public **label-free phase-contrast + deep learning** image set, but melanoma, not immune cells. Also label-free-adjacent: **idr0065-camsund-crispri** (phase contrast + YFP, bacteria). — [IDR projects API](https://idr.openmicroscopy.org/api/v0/m/projects/?limit=600)
- **Broad Bioimage Benchmark Collection — BBBC020, "Murine bone-marrow derived macrophages"**: bone-marrow derived macrophages from **C57BL/6 mice**, used to study the role of **TLR4, Myd88 and MAPK in macrophage spreading** (experiment described in Wenzel et al., 2012); **25 images × 3 channels (DAPI nuclei, CD11b/APC surface, plus a merged image)**; downloads **BBBC020_v1_images.zip (92 MB)**, **BBBC020_v1_outlines_nuclei.zip (2.0 MB)**, **BBBC020_v1_outlines_cells.zip (1.7 MB)**; ground truth = per-cell outlines (incomplete for the nuclei set per a posted complaint); **licence: Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported, by Roland Lang**. **Fluorescent, not label-free; spreading/segmentation benchmark, not polarization.** The BBBC index lists it as the only macrophage/monocyte entry in the collection. — [BBBC020](https://bbbc.broadinstitute.org/BBBC020); [BBBC image sets index](https://bbbc.broadinstitute.org/image_sets)
- **Cell Painting Gallery (broadinstitute/cellpainting-gallery) — no macrophage, monocyte, THP-1 or U937 dataset.** Keyword search of the gallery index found only iPSC- and hepatocyte-type entries, e.g. **cpg0022-cmqtl** (297 iPSC lines; Tegtmeyer et al. 2024; 3.7 TB), **cpg0049-ipsc-diff-pancreatic-progenitor** (17.5 TB), **cpg0048-bsc** (primary human hepatocytes, botanicals, 2.4 TB), **cpg0016-jump** (JUMP-CP, 136,000 perturbations; Chandrasekaran et al., Nat Methods 2024, 21:1114–1121). — [Cell Painting Gallery README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/broadinstitute/cellpainting-gallery/main/README.md)
- **Dryad — the one macrophage-polarization deposit found: doi:10.5061/dryad.1ns1rn8wh**, "Optical signature dataset for living macrophages and monocytes", Dannhauser (Naples Federico II) & Rossi (IIT); **CC0-1.0; 23,964 bytes; 1 version**; underlies the RSOS 2022 light-scattering classifier. **Derived features, not images.** — [Dryad record](https://datadryad.org/api/v2/datasets/doi%3A10.5061%2Fdryad.1ns1rn8wh)
- **Zenodo — keyword searches surface macrophage-polarization *datasets* but none is a label-free imaging set.** Examples: "Dynamics of macrophage polarization in Salmonella infection: Raw data" (10.5281/zenodo.10409519 and 10.5281/zenodo.8031951, CC-BY-4.0, content not verified by me); "Viability and macrophage polarization after the application of mechanical stimulation" (10.5281/zenodo.12189494, CC-BY-4.0); "Dataset for Figure 4 – MDM polarization assay: monocytes differentiation into M1-like macrophages" (10.5281/zenodo.17540731, CC-BY-4.0); "miR-210 promotes the anti-inflammatory phenotype and M2 polarization in murine macrophages" (10.5281/zenodo.18938164, CC-BY-4.0). A query for "macrophage quantitative phase imaging" returned no macrophage QPI dataset. — [Zenodo API](https://zenodo.org/api/records?q=macrophage+polarization+images)
- **Figshare — search inconclusive.** The figshare v2 `articles?search_for=` endpoint returned unranked recent records (e.g. Newcastle journal contributions) for both "macrophage polarization microscopy images" and "macrophage label-free imaging dataset", so I could not enumerate figshare holdings reliably. — [figshare API](https://api.figshare.com/v2/articles)

### Inferences
- The repository sweep is consistent and unambiguous: **public macrophage/monocyte imaging data is dominated by fluorescence (infection, phagocytosis, in vivo tracking) and histology/IMC; label-free activation-state imaging is absent from every major repository.**
- A deposit of label-free M0/M1/M2 (and trained/tolerant) images with paired molecular ground truth would be, as far as these searches show, the first of its kind — the IDR precedent (idr0109, label-free phase contrast + metastatic-potential labels) shows the archives accept exactly this data shape.
- Because BBBC020 is fluorescent and only 25 images, it is unsuitable as a label-free polarization benchmark but is the standard macrophage segmentation benchmark; expect it to be cited as such.

### Gaps
- **Cell Image Library, EMPIAR, Allen Cell and Dryad beyond the one hit were not searched directly** (tool-call budget). I have no evidence either way about macrophage CLEM/cryo-ET in EMPIAR or macrophage sets in the Cell Image Library; Allen Cell's public collection is hiPSC-based and I found no indication of macrophage lines, but I did not verify this against an Allen source.
- Per-accession **size and licence** for the 36 BioImage Archive macrophage studies were not retrieved (the search API does not return them); each would need an individual BioStudies lookup.
- Figshare coverage unresolved (API ranking failure).
- Contents of the two Zenodo "Dynamics of macrophage polarization in Salmonella infection" records (images vs tables) were not inspected.
