# Public time-lapse / video image datasets of immune cells (migration, phagocytosis, morphodynamics)

Scope note: every dataset below was verified by fetching the repository record, the repository's own API, or the paper's data-availability section. Where a technical field (pixel size, total GB, frame count) was **not stated** in the record I fetched, I say so explicitly rather than guessing. Anything I could not verify is flagged in the per-question **Gaps**.

---

## Q1. Accession-level holdings per repository (BioImage Archive, IDR, BBBC, Cell Tracking Challenge, Cell Image Library, Zenodo/Figshare/Dryad, Immunemap, LTDB, DeepCell, LIVECell, Cellpose, CTMC)

### Takeaway
The single best immune-specific, tracking-annotated, openly licensed time-lapse resources are **EBI BioImage Archive S-BIAD2196 "CellTrack"** (human neutrophils, brightfield, OME-Zarr raw + tracked instance masks, CC BY 4.0), the **ComplexEye Zenodo deposit** (186.8 GB of raw neutrophil migration movies, CC BY 4.0), **Immunemap** (400 intravital videos, 58,943 tracks, CC0 data waiver), **LTDB** (38 4D two-photon intravital videos + 728 consensus tracks, Figshare), and **PhagoStat** (94.7 GB microglia phagocytosis video + 1.3 M instance masks, CC BY 4.0). IDR, BBBC and the Cell Tracking Challenge are essentially **empty of innate-immune time-lapse** — their immune holdings are static/high-content, and no CTC sequence is an immune or phagocytic cell type.

### Cited Findings

**EBI BioImage Archive (BioStudies `BioImages` collection) — searched via its REST API**

- **S-BIAD2196 — "CellTrack: Benchmark dataset for 2D cell tracking".** 2D time-lapse of **primary human neutrophil granulocytes** isolated from healthy-donor peripheral blood; acquired on the **ComplexEye multi-lens array microscope**; imaging methods recorded as "time lapse microscopy" + "bright-field microscopy"; contains **raw image data plus instance segmentation masks with tracking information**, label IDs consistent across time, auto-generated then manually curated; **all data in OME-Zarr / OME-NGFF**; **License: CC BY 4.0**; DOI 10.6019/S-BIAD2196; released 2025-07-24; 12 top-level files (Zarr trees count as few "files"). Authors Justin Sonneck, Jianxu Chen (ISAS Dortmund). — [BioStudies API record](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/biostudies/api/v1/studies/S-BIAD2196); [landing page](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/biostudies/BioImages/studies/S-BIAD2196)
- **S-BIAD2133 — "AutoQC-Bench".** >**8,000 brightfield neutrophil-migration images** (single frames extracted from ComplexEye time-lapse videos), from **primary human neutrophils (peripheral blood)** *and* **primary mouse neutrophils (bone marrow)**; includes a curated subset with **manually annotated anomaly segmentation masks** (classes: Air_bubble, Artifacts, Contamination, Illumination, Z-Shift) made in napari, plus `normal_train.csv`/`normal_val.csv` splits; 8,441 files; **License: MIT**; DOI 10.6019/S-BIAD2133; released 2025-07-05. — [BioStudies API record](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/biostudies/api/v1/studies/S-BIAD2133)
- **S-BIAD1871 — "Phagocytosis assay of murine alveolar macrophages with bacteriophage PAK_P1 and fluorescent beads".** Mouse (BALB/c) alveolar macrophages from bronchoalveolar lavage, incubated 0/1/3/5 h with phage PAK_P1 or **0.2 µm FluoSpheres sulfate-modified yellow-green microspheres**, ± 20 µM cytochalasin B; fluorescence microscopy; **612 files**; **License: CC0**; DOI 10.6019/S-BIAD1871; released 2025-04-30. **Important caveat: cells were fixed before imaging** — this is a timepoint series, not a movie. FTP: `https://ftp.ebi.ac.uk/biostudies/fire/S-BIAD/871/S-BIAD1871`. — [BioStudies API record](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/biostudies/api/v1/studies/S-BIAD1871)
- **S-BIAD564 — "In vivo SPIM and confocal imaging of neuronal efferocytosis by microglia".** Raw + processed data underlying Möller et al., eLife 2022 ("A role for the centrosome in regulating the rate of neuronal efferocytosis by microglia in vivo"); **live imaging in the transparent zebrafish embryonic brain**; 5 study components: native microglia; microglia intracellular markers (centrosome, microtubules, actin, early endosomes, lysosomes); photostatin-1 (PST-1) microtubule photo-perturbation; chemical perturbations of microtubules and DAG signalling; CETN4 centrosome-duplication. **680 files**; DOI 10.6019/S-BIAD564; released 2022-11-18. — [BioStudies API record](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/biostudies/api/v1/studies/S-BIAD564)
- **S-BIAD401 — "Characterization of microglia behavior in healthy and pathological conditions with image analysis tools."** Study Type recorded as **"time-lapse imaging"**; *Mus musculus*; primary mixed neuron/glia cultures with **tdTomato-expressing microglia**; keywords include cell shape, segmentation, feature extraction, **phagocytosis**, morphometry, classification, neuroinflammation; **822 files**; released 2022-09-29. Record exposes `Image.dimensions` and `Channel.2.label` columns in its file table. — [BioStudies API record](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/biostudies/api/v1/studies/S-BIAD401)
- **S-BIAD2361** — zebrafish (2 dpf embryos) in-vivo fluorescence imaging of **neutrophil and macrophage clearance** / programmed cell death with *Salmonella* Typhimurium; Leica DFZ7000; **2,472 files**; **CC BY 4.0**; released 2025-11-02. — [BioStudies API record](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/biostudies/api/v1/studies/S-BIAD2361)
- **S-BIAD3375** — "Longitudinal in vivo tracking of macrophage fate using fluorescence imaging"; AmmonChrom7-labelled bone-marrow-derived macrophages, IV/IT transplanted into C57BL/6 mice (chow and GAN/MASH diets); **shortwave-infrared (SWIR) widefield whole-mouse fluorescence** imaging + ex vivo organs; 5 study components; **866 files**; **License: CC0**; DOI 10.6019/S-BIAD3375; released 2026-10-01. This is macroscopic organ-level biodistribution, **not** cellular morphodynamics. — [BioStudies API record](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/biostudies/api/v1/studies/S-BIAD3375)
- **S-BIAD452** — "Imaging flow cytometry dataset of healthy human white blood cells" (Lippeveld et al.); IFC, up to 12 channels including **brightfield + darkfield**; granulocytes/eosinophils/neutrophils/lymphocytes; 45 files; released 2022-05-17. Single-cell snapshots, **not** time-lapse. — [BioStudies API record](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/biostudies/api/v1/studies/S-BIAD452)
- **S-BIAD829** — "Trained immunity induced by high salt diet impedes stroke recovery"; mouse brain immunofluorescence/IHC of monocyte-derived macrophages, Zeiss Axio Imager M1 20x/0.8 and Olympus IX81, 1360 × 1024 px; **67 files**; **CC0**; released 2023-08-31. Directly on-topic for *trained immunity* but **fixed sections, not video**. — [BioStudies API record](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/biostudies/api/v1/studies/S-BIAD829)
- **S-BIAD3674 — "ChemoTrack"** (useful substitute, non-immune): raw phase-contrast time-lapse + single-cell tracks of ***Dictyostelium discoideum*** acaA− cells in Insall chemotaxis chambers under **precisely quantified cAMP-analogue (Sp-cAMPS) gradients** (folders named e.g. `Cmin-0-Cmax-250nM`, `Cmin-0-Cmax-10uM`, `Cmin-1mM-Cmax-1mM`); Nikon Ti2, 10x, MOMENT-MONO sCMOS; **frame interval 30 s**, **pixel size 0.45 µm/px**, TIFF; **1,467 files**; **CC BY 4.0**; DOI 10.6019/S-BIAD3674; released 2026-06-27. Dictyostelium is the canonical amoeboid-chemotaxis surrogate for neutrophils. — [BioStudies API record](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/biostudies/api/v1/studies/S-BIAD3674)
- Other BioImage Archive immune-adjacent records surfaced by the API, by keyword count: `microglia` 23 hits, `phagocytosis` 9 hits, `neutrophil` 17 hits, `monocyte` 5 hits, `leukocyte` 3 hits, `intravital two-photon` 31 hits. Notable additional accessions: **S-BIAD849** (candidalysin-induced NETs, 363 files), **S-BIAD2463** (pyroptosis in *M. avium*/*M. marinum*, 18 files), **S-BSST698** (autophagy and host defence in a zebrafish invasive-aspergillosis model, 4 files), **S-BIAD3811** (CD24 innate-immune evasion, zebrafish xenografts, 10 files), **S-BIAD2500/2498/2499/2502/2503** (microglia morphology / synapse engulfment / microglia–neuron contacts after TIA, 4–49 files each), **S-BIAD2344** ("Image-based profiling of healthy donor immune cells with Blood Cell Painting", **577,256 files** — fixed Cell Painting, not video), **S-BIAD860/idr0026** (anti-CD137 CTL killer response, OME-NGFF, 111 files), **S-BIAD2705** (multi-structure 3D multiphoton liver microscopy incl. real + physics-based + GAN-simulated volumes, 23,149 files). — [BioStudies search API](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/biostudies/api/v1/search?query=microglia&collection=BioImages)

**Image Data Resource (IDR) — enumerated via its own API (`/api/v0/m/projects/?limit=600` → 147 projects; `/api/v0/m/screens/?limit=600` → 105 screens)**

- Keyword scan of all 147 IDR projects + 105 screens returned **no innate-immune time-lapse study**. The only immune-cell studies are: **idr0026-weigelin-immunotherapy/experimentA** (adoptive CTL transfer + agonist anti-CD137 mAb — intravital imaging of cytotoxic T lymphocytes, i.e. adaptive not innate), **idr0054-segura-tonsilhyperion/experimentA** (imaging mass cytometry of tonsil, "cDC2 and macrophages play complementary roles in T follicular helper responses" — static IMC), **idr0120-german-immunologicalsynapse/screenA–D** ("Morphological profiling of human T and NK lymphocytes by high-content cell imaging": Jurkat, NK92, **primary NK cells**, patient-derived cells — high-content, not time-lapse), **idr0073-schaadt-immuneinfiltrates** (graph description of tertiary lymphoid organs, static), **idr0082-pennycuick-lesions** (immune surveillance in pre-invasive lung SCC, H&E). Infection screens exist (idr0081 adenovirus, idr0128 influenza, idr0129 rhinovirus, idr0130 herpes) but in epithelial lines. — [IDR projects API](https://idr.openmicroscopy.org/api/v0/m/projects/?limit=600); [IDR screens API](https://idr.openmicroscopy.org/api/v0/m/screens/?limit=600)
- IDR's `searchengine` REST endpoint rejects `all` and `container` as resource names (`{"Error":"all is not a valid resource"}`), so the project/screen API enumeration above is the reliable route. — [IDR searchengine attempt](https://idr.openmicroscopy.org/searchengine/api/v1/resources/all/search/?key=any&value=macrophage)
- IDR scale for context: >13 million images, >300 TB, served through OMERO with web UI + APIs. — [GerBI featured-repository overview](https://gerbi-gmb.de/2023/06/02/featured-repository-image-data-resource/)

**Broad Bioimage Benchmark Collection (BBBC) — 55 unique image sets enumerated from the index**

- Immune-relevant sets: **BBBC020** (murine bone-marrow-derived macrophages; 25 three-channel sets, DAPI nuclei + CD11b/APC surface; ground truth = per-cell outlines for overlapping-cell segmentation; study focus TLR4/Myd88/MAPK-dependent macrophage spreading; **note the site's own caveat that `BBB020_v1_outlines_nuclei` may be incomplete**), **BBBC045** (human white blood cells), **BBBC054** (LPS-activated immortalized mouse microglia — see Q6). Infection sets: **BBBC041** (*P. vivax*-infected blood smears), **BBBC012** (*C. elegans* infection marker). Hematopoietic-lineage but synthetic/static: **BBBC024** (3D HL60, synthetic), **BBBC035** (simulated HL60 from the Cell Tracking Challenge), **BBBC048** (cell-cycle Jurkat cells). — [BBBC image-set index](https://bbbc.broadinstitute.org/image_sets); [BBBC020](https://bbbc.broadinstitute.org/BBBC020)
- **No BBBC set is described as phagocytosis**, and **none of the 55 sets is explicitly described as time-lapse/video** (BBBC019 "collective cell migration" is the only possible candidate and the index does not say so). BBBC is cited in 500+ studies; original descriptor Ljosa, Sokolnicki & Carpenter, *Nature Methods* 2012, doi:10.1038/nmeth.2083. — [BBBC image-set index](https://bbbc.broadinstitute.org/image_sets)

**Cell Tracking Challenge (CTC)**

- The 2D+Time catalogue has **10 datasets**, and **none involves immune or phagocytic cells**. Full verified table: BF-C2DL-HSC (mouse hematopoietic stem cells in hydrogel microwells; brightfield; **0.645 µm/px**; **5 min**; 1.6 GB train / 1.6 GB test); BF-C2DL-MuSC (mouse muscle stem cells; 0.645 µm/px; 5 min; 1.2/1.3 GB); DIC-C2DH-HeLa (DIC; 0.19 µm/px; 10 min; 37/41 MB); Fluo-C2DL-Huh7 (YFP-TIA-1 hepatocarcinoma; 0.65 µm/px; 15 min; 36/36 MB); Fluo-C2DL-MSC (rat MSC; 0.3 / 0.3977 µm/px; 20 / 30 min; 72/71 MB); Fluo-N2DH-GOWT1 (GFP-GOWT1 mouse ES; 0.240 µm/px; 5 min; 53/46 MB); Fluo-N2DL-HeLa (H2b-GFP; 0.645 µm/px; 30 min; 182/168 MB); PhC-C2DH-U373 (glioblastoma-astrocytoma on polyacrylamide; 0.65 µm/px; 15 min; 40/38 MB); PhC-C2DL-PSC (pancreatic stem cells; 1.6 µm/px; 10 min; 124/106 MB); Fluo-N2DH-SIM+ (simulated Hoechst-stained **HL60** nuclei; 0.125 µm/px; 29 min; 91/96 MB). Silver reference segmentations exist for BF-C2DL-HSC, BF-C2DL-MuSC, DIC-C2DH-HeLa, Fluo-C2DL-MSC, Fluo-N2DH-GOWT1, Fluo-N2DL-HeLa, PhC-C2DL-PSC; Fluo-N2DH-SIM+ has perfect linking masks. **The page states no license** (only "Cell Tracking Challenge © 2012-2024" + provider credits). — [CTC 2D datasets](https://celltrackingchallenge.net/2d-datasets/)
- Closest immune proxy: **Fluo-N2DH-SIM+** and **BBBC035/BBBC024** are simulated **HL60** (promyelocytic leukemia) nuclei — a myeloid line, but synthetic nuclei only; **BF-C2DL-HSC** is hematopoietic stem cells (blood-forming progenitors, not differentiated innate immune cells). — [CTC 2D datasets](https://celltrackingchallenge.net/2d-datasets/)
- **PhC-C2DH-U373** independently reported as **115 frames per sequence at 696 × 520 px**, sparse cells, bright background. — [Cell as Point, arXiv 2411.14833](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2411.14833); corroborated by [GNN cell tracking, arXiv 2202.04731](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2202.04731)
- **Fluo-C3DL-MDA231** (3D fluorescence, MDA231 breast carcinoma, irregular shapes with thin protrusions) received **full 3D manual segmentation annotations for two time-lapse sequences** (one annotated by three annotators, majority-vote fused) in a 2025 release. — [arXiv 2510.10797](https://www.arxiv.org/pdf/2510.10797)
- 10-year challenge retrospective (for provenance/licensing language): — [The Cell Tracking Challenge: 10 years of objective benchmarking, PMC10333123](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10333123/)

**Leukocyte Tracking Database (LTDB) — Pizzagalli et al., *Scientific Data* 5:180129 (2018)**

- Hosted on **Figshare, collection DOI 10.6084/m9.figshare.c.3827890**; article DOI **10.1038/sdata.2018.129**; web interface at **ltdb.info**. **License: article CC BY 4.0; CC0 waiver on the metadata files; MATLAB code GPL v3.**
- **38 videos total: 20 main entries (LTDB001–LTDB020) + 18 case studies (CS001–CS018), from 26 unique experiments.**
- Modality: **two-photon intravital microscopy (MP-IVM)**, **4D (3D volumes over time)**, three microscope platforms. Species **mouse**, including **BLT NOD.scid humanized mice**. Organs: **popliteal lymph node and spleen**.
- Technical: **frame interval ~12–60 s**; **xy voxel ~0.27–1.15 µm, z ~2–4 µm**; width/height **~69–725 px**, **10–22 z-planes**, **20–242 time points**; 8/12/14-bit stored as uint16; **1–4 channels**.
- Annotations: **728 consensus ground-truth tracks = 44,722 centroid annotations (~61 per track, ~260 cell-hours)**; three operators produced **1,850 tracks / 113,807 annotations** in total.
- Cell types **tracked**: **neutrophils, T cells (incl. HIV-infected humanized CD4+ T cells), B cells, NK cells**. **Dendritic cells and macrophages are visible in some channels but were NOT tracked.**
- Formats: TIFF image series, CSV track files, an SQL database dump, MP4 previews, PNG snapshots, MATLAB scripts. **Total data volume is not stated in the paper.**
- — [LTDB, PMC6049032](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6049032/); [Figshare collection 3827890](https://figshare.com/collections/Leukocyte_Tracking_Database_a_collection_of_immune_cell_tracks_from_intravital_2-photon_microscopy_videos/3827890); [Europe PMC record](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/search?query=%22Leukocyte%20Tracking%20Database%22&format=json&resultType=core)
- Caveat on the Figshare landing page: it returned **HTTP 403** to my automated fetch (works in a browser); the collection DOI from the paper is the authoritative pointer. Internal validation in the paper compared automatic vs manual tracks for entry **LTDB017a** and found significantly different track length and duration. — [UPF repository record](https://repositori.upf.edu/items/47f4699b-6fab-44a0-b570-cb1b5ad39665)

**Immunemap / immunemap.org — Pizzagalli et al., *EMBO J* 45(1):334–348 (2026), DOI 10.1038/s44318-025-00629-4**

- **>400 intravital microscopy videos** of **murine** models; **233 manually tracked in 2D** yielding **58,943 tracks** and **1,049,340 centroid annotations**.
- Per-cell-type track counts: **T cells 25,573** (CD4+ 10,347; CD8+ 13,056), **neutrophils 11,047**, **NK cells 6,244**, **B cells 2,295**, **innate lymphoid cells 1,952**, **eosinophils 894**, **dendritic cells 241**; platelets and humanized-mouse cells also included. **Macrophages are not reported as a tracked population.**
- Tissues: **trachea, lung, spleen, skin, spinal cord, lymph node, liver**, plus lymphatic and blood vessels. Modality: intravital **multiphoton** and **confocal spinning-disk**.
- Access: cloud architecture with interactive web viewer plus **public APIs delivering videos, metadata and tracks**; Figure 6 states **"direct download options for raw and processed data."** Formats: **videos HDF5, metadata JSON, tracks CSV + JSON**; documented integration with **CelltrackR (R), Python, MATLAB**.
- **License: article CC BY 4.0; the paper states the CC0 public-domain waiver applies to the associated data** (excluding graphical elements). Total data volume not stated.
- 3D validation in the paper used "a previously published dataset of manually annotated 3D tracks" cited to Pizzagalli et al. 2018 — i.e. **Immunemap is the successor/superset of LTDB**, though LTDB is not named in the body text.
- — [Immunemap paper, PMC12759063](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12759063/); [bioRxiv preprint 2024.12.02.626343](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2024.12.02.626343.full.pdf); [PubMed 41266653](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41266653/)

**Zenodo / Figshare / Dryad**

- **ComplexEye raw neutrophil movies — Zenodo DOI 10.5281/zenodo.7962144 (concept), v2 record 10.5281/zenodo.10082674**, published 2023-11-07. **186.8 GB across 21 ZIP archives**, **CC BY 4.0**, 25 creators (Cibir, Gunzer, Viga et al., Univ. Hospital Essen / ISAS / Lead Discovery Center). Described as "raw high-resolution movies of migrating neutrophils" from the 16-lens array microscope; archives correspond to Figure 3a (96-well, n=1, ComplexEye + Leica), Figure 3b (384-well, n=1), Figure 3c (384-well, n=16), and **R01–R17** (Figures 4–5). Container video format inside the ZIPs is not stated on the record. — [Zenodo 7962144 / 10082674](https://zenodo.org/records/7962144)
- ComplexEye acquisition parameters from the paper (Nat Commun 14, 2023, DOI 10.1038/s41467-023-43765-3): **1,042 movies** in the compound screen (Fig. 4 legend says 1,000), **>1.2 million neutrophils tracked (mean 1,245 per movie)**, **1 frame / 8 s**, **most recordings 1 hour**, **1280 × 800 px 8-bit grayscale**, field of view **825.8 × 512 µm** (→ ~0.65 µm/px, my calculation, not stated); **primary human neutrophils from healthy volunteers** are the main subject, with mouse cutaneous melanoma and murine CT26 colorectal carcinoma cells also imaged; tracking code at `MMV-Lab/complex_eye_analysis`. The paper screened **1,000 compounds, finding 17 modifiers of human neutrophil migration, in 4 days** (~60× faster than conventional video microscopy). Data availability verbatim: *"To reproduce our results, raw data of all movies tracked in the frame of this manuscript … have been deposited at 10.5281/zenodo.7962144. Source data are provided with this paper."* **The paper does not explicitly state that per-cell tracking annotations are released** — the Zenodo deposit is raw movies; curated tracks come via S-BIAD2196. — [ComplexEye, PMC10713721](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10713721/)
- **PhagoStat — "Microglia video microscopy dataset v1", Zenodo record 10803492**, published 2024-03-10; Ounissi, Latouche, Racoceanu; paper *Scientific Reports* DOI 10.1038/s41598-024-56081-7 (arXiv 2304.13764); code `github.com/ounissimehdi/PhagoStat`. **License CC BY 4.0.** **Total 94.7 GB**: `microglia_video_microscopy_dataset_v1.zip` 94.2 GB + `manually_annotated_data.zip` 497.3 MB. Content: **wild-type and FTD (frontotemporal-dementia) microglia** phagocytosing aggregates; **20 distinct scenes per acquisition, 7 hours of time-lapse per acquisition**; **36,496 normalized cell/aggregate images; 36,496 registered aggregates; 1,306,131 cell instance masks; 1,553,036 aggregate instance masks**; frame-by-frame biological features (area, position, speed); **226 manually annotated images with 6,430 individual cell masks**. Frame interval is **not stated** on the Zenodo record. — [Zenodo 10803492](https://zenodo.org/records/10803492)
- **Zebrafish macrophage morpho-kinetics — Zenodo DOI 10.5281/zenodo.16319732** (Seol Ah Park, STU Bratislava), published 2025-07-22. `Raw_MaxProjected_Videos.zip` **10.7 GB** + `Processed_Data.zip` 1.3 MB (extracted trajectories, cell area, perimeter, distance from wound). **License: CC BY-NC-ND 4.0** (note: no-derivatives — a real constraint for model training/redistribution). — [Zenodo 16319732](https://zenodo.org/records/16319732)
- **Dryad doi:10.5061/dryad.m0cfxpp8v** — "A data-driven approach to establishing cell motility patterns as predictors of macrophage subtypes and their relation to cell morphology" (Yaohui Sun, UC Davis; Manasa Kesapragada, UCSC), published 2025-10-25. **2.23 GB**, **18 multi-page TIFF time-lapse files + README**, grouped **M0 (4 files), M1 (6), M2 (8)**; murine bone-marrow-derived macrophages from **20 mice in 5 batches**; **M1 = 100 ng/ml LPS, M2 = 20 ng/ml IL-4**; **phase contrast**; **frame interval 5 min for all files**; **10× = 2.155 px/µm (batch 1 "16H" files), 20× = 4.31 px/µm (all others)**; collected Sept 2019 – May 2021; batch 5 used different wells and only M1/M2. Dryad does not name a specific license on the record beyond "licensed for reuse" + Dryad terms (Dryad default is CC0). — [Dryad m0cfxpp8v](https://datadryad.org/dataset/doi:10.5061/dryad.m0cfxpp8v)
- **Dryad doi:10.5061/dryad.1ns1rn8wh** — "Optical signature dataset for living macrophages and monocytes": label-free **light-scattering** signatures for M0/M1/M2 macrophages and naive monocytes, >85% average classification accuracy. **Not image/video data** — numeric optical signatures. — [Dryad 1ns1rn8wh](https://datadryad.org/dataset/doi:10.5061/dryad.1ns1rn8wh)
- **Synthetic zebrafish neutrophil tracking datasets**, City University of London, deposited on Zenodo 2013 (records **1221031, 1221032, 1221162**): MATLAB-generated synthetic neutrophil movies with **ground-truth trajectory files**, built to test segmentation/tracking; associated with the **PhagoSight** MATLAB package for fluorescent neutrophil and macrophage migration in zebrafish. — [Zenodo 1221032](https://zenodo.org/records/1221032); [City U. record](https://openaccess.city.ac.uk/id/eprint/4317); [PhagoSight paper, PMC3758287](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3758287/)
- **Zenodo full-text search is unreliable for this via the public API**: multi-word queries (`q=macrophage time-lapse`, `q=phagocytosis time-lapse dataset`, etc.) returned only the most-recent unrelated records rather than keyword matches. Zenodo coverage here should be treated as **incomplete** — the records above were found via the literature, not via repository search.

**CTMC (Cell Tracking with Mitosis Detection Challenge)**

- **CTMC-v1**: **86 live-cell imaging videos** (47 train / 39 test) across **14 cell lines**; **~152,498 frames total** (80,389 train + 72,109 test — the sum is my arithmetic; the page lists the two separately); **400 × 320 px**; **30 FPS** as listed; annotations are **human-drawn bounding boxes with track IDs** (ground-truth videos for training sequences only). **License: CC BY-NC-SA 3.0**, with the caveat that third-party-sourced sequences may carry their original conditions. - Origin: Anjum & Gurari, CVPRW 2020.

### Inferences
- The immune-cell video "ecosystem" is bimodal: (a) *in vitro* **brightfield neutrophil migration at industrial scale** (ComplexEye lineage: Zenodo raw movies + S-BIAD2133 frames + S-BIAD2196 curated tracks — all from the Gunzer/Chen Essen–ISAS axis), and (b) ***in vivo* two-photon/spinning-disk intravital** (LTDB → Immunemap, Switzerland/USI–Bern axis). There is almost nothing in the classic CV benchmark repositories (IDR/BBBC/CTC).
- For **point tracking** specifically, Immunemap's 1,049,340 centroid annotations and LTDB's 44,722 consensus annotations are the only large bodies of *point-level* immune-cell ground truth; everything else is masks or boxes. Centroid-only annotation is actually the natural supervision format for point-tracking evaluation, so these two are the highest-value targets.
- S-BIAD2196 (CellTrack) is the best single "drop-in" training set: OME-Zarr, consistent label IDs over time, CC BY 4.0, real innate immune cells, brightfield (so no fluorophore-specific appearance prior).
- Because CTC/BBBC/CTMC contain no immune cells, any immune-cell video model will have to either (i) pretrain on general live-cell corpora and fine-tune on the handful of immune sets above, or (ii) treat *Dictyostelium* (S-BIAD3674) as the amoeboid-motility surrogate.

### Gaps
- **Total data volume in GB/TB is not published** for S-BIAD2196, S-BIAD2133, S-BIAD564, S-BIAD401, S-BIAD2361, S-BIAD1871, S-BIAD3674 — BioStudies' `/info` endpoint returns file counts and FTP/Globus links but the byte totals were not in the JSON I retrieved. They are obtainable by listing `https://ftp.ebi.ac.uk/biostudies/fire/S-BIAD/<last3>/<accession>/`.
- **Frame interval, number of movies, movie duration, and image dimensions are not stated** on any of the BioImage Archive records for S-BIAD2196, S-BIAD2133 (beyond the ComplexEye instrument defaults of 8 s / 1 h / 1280×800 inherited from the Nat Commun paper), S-BIAD2361, S-BIAD401, S-BIAD564.
- **Immunemap's total data volume, exact API endpoints, and per-video technical parameters** are not in the paper; immunemap.org itself was not fetched (it is a JS app).
- **LTDB total volume** is not stated anywhere I could verify; the Figshare landing page blocks automated fetching (HTTP 403).
- **Cell Image Library** could not be systematically searched (TLS failure). Its holdings beyond CIL:35191 are unverified.
- **Zenodo and Figshare are almost certainly under-covered** here, because their keyword search APIs did not behave as documented for multi-word queries.
- No **"EPFL/ETH immune imaging release"** was found under that description. I found no EPFL- or ETH-branded public immune time-lapse dataset. Treat that item in the brief as **unconfirmed/likely nonexistent** unless the requester has a specific citation. (The Swiss intravital immune-imaging open data that does exist is the **USI Lugano / Univ. Bern** LTDB–Immunemap line, not EPFL/ETH.)
- **Releases through 2026**: the latest relevant items I verified are S-BIAD3674 (2026-06-27), S-BIAD3375 (2026-10-01), S-BIAD2463 (2026-04-30), S-BIAD3811 (2026-07-20) and the Immunemap EMBO J paper (Jan 2026). I found no 2026 immune-specific *video benchmark* release.

## Q2. Intravital two-photon leukocyte/neutrophil/macrophage migration — actually downloadable, and under what terms?

### Takeaway
Two resources, both from the same Swiss group lineage, are genuinely downloadable: **LTDB** (Figshare, 38 4D MP-IVM videos + 728 consensus tracks; CC BY 4.0 article, CC0 metadata, GPLv3 code) and **Immunemap** (>400 intravital videos, 58,943 tracks, HDF5/CSV/JSON via public APIs with "direct download options for raw and processed data", with the paper stating a **CC0 waiver on the data**). Everything else intravital that I could verify is figure-support data rather than a tracking corpus.

### Cited Findings
- LTDB: **two-photon intravital microscopy (MP-IVM)**, mouse + BLT NOD.scid humanized mouse, popliteal lymph node and spleen, **4D**, 38 videos / 26 experiments, **12–60 s frame interval**, xy 0.27–1.15 µm, z 2–4 µm, 20–242 time points, 10–22 z-planes, TIFF + CSV + SQL dump + MP4 previews; **728 consensus tracks / 44,722 annotations**; tracked cells = neutrophils, T cells, B cells, NK cells (**macrophages and DCs visible but untracked**). Figshare collection **10.6084/m9.figshare.c.3827890**, web interface **ltdb.info**. — [PMC6049032](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6049032/)
- Immunemap: multiphoton + confocal spinning-disk intravital, murine, 7 tissues + vessels; **11,047 neutrophil tracks** and **241 dendritic-cell tracks** among 58,943 total; videos in **HDF5**, tracks in **CSV/JSON**, metadata JSON; public APIs; CelltrackR/Python/MATLAB integration; **CC0 waiver stated for the data**. — [PMC12759063](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12759063/)
- IDR **idr0026-weigelin-immunotherapy/experimentA** — intravital imaging of adoptively transferred cytotoxic T lymphocytes under agonist anti-CD137 mAb; also mirrored in BioImage Archive as **S-BIAD860** ("Focusing and sustaining the antitumor CTL effector killer response by agonist anti-CD137 mAb (OME-NGFF)", 111 files, released 2023-08-23). Adaptive (CTL), not innate. — [IDR projects API](https://idr.openmicroscopy.org/api/v0/m/projects/?limit=600); [BioStudies search](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/biostudies/api/v1/search?query=intravital%20two-photon&collection=BioImages)
- Other intravital BioImage Archive records (not immune-migration corpora): **S-BIAD2266** (digital expansion microscopy for intravital imaging, 465 files), **S-BIAD1186** (serial two-photon tomography of *Salmonella*-infected mouse spleen, 4,260 files — fixed/cleared tomography, not live), **S-BIAD2705** (3D multiphoton liver, real + physics-based + GAN-simulated volumes, 23,149 files), **S-BIAD1572** (CD4-mediated immunity shapes neutrophil-driven tuberculous pathology, 113 files), **S-BSST147** (megakaryocyte migration into bone-marrow vessels, 11 files). — [BioStudies search](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/biostudies/api/v1/search?query=intravital%20two-photon&collection=BioImages)
- Immunemap's stated motivation is precisely that intravital microscopy data is otherwise "mostly kept within individual labs." — [PMC12759063](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12759063/)

### Inferences
- For 3D/4D point tracking of innate immune cells in vivo, **LTDB is the only dataset with published per-frame voxel sizes, frame intervals and multi-operator consensus tracks** — i.e. the only one where tracking error can be calibrated against inter-annotator variance (1,850 operator tracks vs 728 consensus tracks gives an explicit human-agreement floor).
- Immunemap supersedes LTDB in scale by ~70× in tracks but is **2D-tracked** (233 of 400 videos, "manually tracked in 2D"), so its annotations are projections; LTDB remains the 3D ground truth. For a point-tracking model that works on max-projected 2D frames, Immunemap is the right target; for 3D, LTDB.

### Gaps
- Whether Immunemap's raw HDF5 videos require account registration is **not stated** in the paper; the paper says "direct download options" and "public APIs", which implies no gate, but I could not test immunemap.org itself. **Flag as unverified.**
- No macrophage intravital tracking corpus exists in either resource (macrophages are visible but untracked in LTDB; not reported in Immunemap).
- Total bytes for both are unpublished.

## Q3. Zebrafish neutrophil/macrophage migration and phagocytosis time-lapse

Zebrafish is the richest *in vivo* innate-immune video source, and several deposits are genuinely downloadable: **S-BIAD564** (microglial efferocytosis of apoptotic neurons, SPIM + confocal, 680 files), **Zenodo 16319732** (20 wound-healing macrophage videos with trajectories and TNF-based M1/M2/M0 labels, 10.7 GB, but **CC BY-NC-ND**), **S-BIAD2361** (neutrophil + macrophage clearance vs *Salmonella*, 2,472 files, CC BY 4.0), and the **PhagoSight** synthetic neutrophil sets with ground-truth trajectories.

### Cited Findings
- **Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.16319732** + arXiv 2507.19314 ("The Morpho-Kinetic Landscape of Macrophage Modes During Wound Healing in Zebrafish", Park et al.): **20 time-lapse videos** — M1: 5, M2: 2, transition period: 5, Non-M1: 5, M0: 3. **In vivo intravital confocal spinning-disk** (ANDOR CSU-W1 on inverted Nikon Ti Eclipse, ANDOR Neo sCMOS, **20× air NA 0.75**, 488 nm + 561 nm, **z-step 1–3 µm**, max-intensity-projected to 2D). **Pixel size 0.32 µm/px** (M1/M2/transition/Non-M1) and **0.31 µm/px** (M0). **Time step 1–2.5 min** (1.5 min for M0). Tracked macrophages/trajectories: **M1 3,308 / 55; M2 3,034 / 60; transition 5,743 / 113; Non-M1 5,266 / 118; M0 9,917 / 132** (totals: 27,268 macrophage detections, 478 trajectories). - **S-BIAD564** — live SPIM + confocal imaging of microglia engulfing apoptotic neurons in the zebrafish embryonic brain (Möller et al., eLife 2022); 5 perturbation arms incl. photoswitchable microtubule destabiliser PST-1; 680 files; DOI 10.6019/S-BIAD564. — [BioStudies](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/biostudies/api/v1/studies/S-BIAD564)

- **S-BSST698** — autophagic-lysosomal axis and host defence in a zebrafish invasive-aspergillosis model (4 files); **S-BIAD2463** — pyroptosis in *M. avium* / *M. marinum* infections, Meijer/Spink lab (18 files, released 2026-04-30); **S-BIAD3811** — CD24-driven innate immune evasion, zebrafish xenografts (10 files, 2026-07-20); **S-BIAD796** — "Opto-inflammasome in zebrafish" tool for cell/tissue responses to speck formation and cell death (1,628 files). — [BioStudies search, neutrophil](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/biostudies/api/v1/search?query=neutrophil&collection=BioImages)
- Literature leads with movies in supplementary (not deposited as datasets): a 2025 bioRxiv light-sheet study tracking **both neutrophils and macrophages in 3D after caudal fin transection in 5 dpf Tg(mpx:GFP; mpeg1:mCherry) larvae**; Barros-Becker et al., *J Cell Sci* 2017 (random vs wound-directed neutrophil and macrophage migration, 3 dpf); a 2022 *J Cell Biol* two-photon study of neutrophils in Tg(mpx:Lifeact-Ruby) after laser wounding; a review of neutrophil reverse migration imaging in zebrafish. — [bioRxiv 2025.08.12.669957](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.08.12.669957.full.pdf); [PMC8855992 review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8855992)
- **CIL:35191** — zebrafish video of early macrophages phagocytosing injected live *E. coli* and *B. subtilis*. — [UCSD DC record](https://library.ucsd.edu/dc/object/bb13036950)

- Zenodo 16319732 is the **best-characterised immune time-lapse dataset anywhere** in terms of published technical metadata (per-condition pixel size, time step, duration window, detection and trajectory counts) — but its **CC BY-NC-ND licence is the most restrictive of the whole catalogue**, which matters if derived model weights or re-processed frames would count as derivatives. - 478 trajectories / 27,268 detections is small for supervised tracking but ideal as a **held-out evaluation set** for a model pretrained self-supervised on ComplexEye + PhagoStat.
- Zebrafish supplementary movies are abundant in the literature but mostly **not deposited** — the gap between "published movies" and "downloadable dataset" is the main bottleneck for this modality.

### Gaps
- The 2025 light-sheet neutrophil+macrophage bioRxiv study's data accession (if any) was not confirmed; its movies may be supplementary-only.
- I could not verify total volume, frame counts or pixel sizes for S-BIAD564, S-BIAD2361, S-BSST698, S-BIAD2463, S-BIAD3811 — these records carry no acquisition-parameter fields beyond instrument name.

## Q4. Microglia live-imaging datasets (in vitro and in vivo)

### Takeaway
Microglia are unusually well served: **PhagoStat** (94.7 GB, 1.3 M cell instance masks, CC BY 4.0) for in vitro phagocytosis video, **S-BIAD401** (mouse primary tdTomato microglia, Study Type = time-lapse imaging, 822 files) for in vitro morphodynamics, **S-BIAD564** for in vivo zebrafish efferocytosis, **BBBC054** for LPS-activation phenotype labels, and **LIVECell's BV-2 line** for large-scale label-free microglia segmentation.

### Cited Findings
- **PhagoStat / Zenodo 10803492**: wild-type and FTD microglia; **20 scenes per acquisition, 7 h time-lapse per acquisition**; **1,306,131 cell instance masks + 1,553,036 aggregate instance masks**; 36,496 normalized cell/aggregate images; frame-by-frame area/position/speed; **226 manually annotated images / 6,430 cell masks**; **94.7 GB**; **CC BY 4.0**; paper DOI 10.1038/s41598-024-56081-7. — [Zenodo 10803492](https://zenodo.org/records/10803492)

- **S-BIAD564**: in vivo SPIM + confocal live imaging of microglial efferocytosis in zebrafish, 680 files (see Q3). — [BioStudies](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/biostudies/api/v1/studies/S-BIAD564)
- **BBBC054**: LPS-activated immortalized mouse microglia (IMG); Leica DMI 6000, **20× objective**, Andor AND-DG-152V-C1E-FI camera, Metamorph Premiere, TIFF; **each field of view corresponds to an LPS exposure time, sampled every 30 min from 0 to 30 hours**; ground truth CSV with columns `index, axis-0 (FOV/image), axis-1 (x), axis-2 (y), label` where **label ∈ {round, amoeboid, stratified}** (manually annotated morphological phenotypes); downloads = Replicate 1 ZIP + Replicate 1 annotation CSV + Replicate 2 ZIP + Replicate 3 ZIP; **License CC BY 3.0 Unported (commercial use allowed)**; created at Caltech BBE, contact Valentine Svensson. - **LIVECell** includes **BV-2 mouse microglia** as one of its 8 cell lines (the only immune-lineage line in LIVECell). — [PMC8440198](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8440198/)
- In vivo mouse microglia morphology/engulfment records in BioImage Archive (fixed, not live): **S-BIAD2500** (morphology after ATP brain injection, 49 files), **S-BIAD2499** (synapse engulfment 24 h after TIA, 20 files), **S-BIAD2498** (microglia–neuron contacts 24 h after TIA, 21 files), **S-BIAD2502/2503** (± P2Y12 inhibitor), **S-BIAD2332** ("Unmasking Early Microglial Remodeling in an Alzheimer's Disease Mouse Model", 21 files), **S-BSST1009** / **S-BSST609** (microglial phagocytosis / refinement of spinal circuits, 11 and 10 files). — [BioStudies search, microglia](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/biostudies/api/v1/search?query=microglia&collection=BioImages)

### Inferences
- **PhagoStat is the single largest immune-cell video corpus with dense instance masks that is openly licensed.** Its 1.3 M cell masks + 1.55 M aggregate masks across 7-hour recordings make it the natural backbone for self-supervised video pretraining on phagocytic morphodynamics, and the WT-vs-FTD split gives a free coarse "genotype state" label.
- BBBC054 is the closest thing in BBBC to an activation-state-labelled time series, but because each FOV is a different exposure time, it supports *classification across a time axis* rather than *tracking within a movie*.

### Gaps
- PhagoStat's **frame interval** is not on the Zenodo record (only "7 hours" and "20 scenes"); it is presumably in the *Scientific Reports* paper or GitHub, which I did not fetch.
- BBBC054's image count, dimensions and ZIP sizes are not published on its page.
- S-BIAD401's frame interval, number of movies and pixel size are not in the record.

## Q6. Datasets with BOTH time-lapse video AND a per-cell activation-state label

### Takeaway
Three candidates, and the distinction between *per-cell* and *per-condition* labelling matters a lot. **Zenodo 16319732** (zebrafish macrophages) is the only one with genuinely per-cell, per-trajectory activation labels derived from a live reporter (TNF-GFP) inside the movie. **BBBC054** has per-cell *morphological phenotype* labels with (x, y, FOV) coordinates across an LPS time course but is sampled FOVs, not a tracked movie. **Dryad m0cfxpp8v** has true 5-min-interval movies but labels are **per-file/per-condition** (M0/M1/M2 stimulation), not per cell.

### Cited Findings
- **Zenodo 16319732 / arXiv 2507.19314**: labels assigned per macrophage from the **Tg(tnfa:GFP-F)ump5** reporter plus time after amputation — **M1** = tnfa:GFP-positive within the first 6 hpa; **M2** = macrophages transitioning from M1, observed 10.5–16 hpa; **Non-M1** = no TNF expression within the first 6 hpa; **M0** = homeostatic macrophages in unwounded larvae; **transition** = TNF expression during 6–10.5 hpa. The authors state explicitly that **no specific M2 marker was available**, so M2 is inferred from reporter history + time window, not a direct marker. Per-class trajectory counts: M1 55, M2 60, transition 113, Non-M1 118, M0 132 trajectories (3,308 / 3,034 / 5,743 / 5,266 / 9,917 macrophage detections respectively). The classification of transition-period cells into "M1-like and M2-like" groups was done **from morpho-kinetic features**, i.e. the labels for that subset are partly model-derived. Licence **CC BY-NC-ND 4.0**. — [arXiv 2507.19314 HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.19314v1); [arXiv abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.19314); [Zenodo 16319732](https://zenodo.org/records/16319732)
- **BBBC054**: ground-truth CSV gives, per row, a **cell location (x, y) plus FOV index plus a manually annotated phenotype label ∈ {round, amoeboid, stratified}**, across LPS exposure times sampled every 30 min from 0–30 h; the stated purpose is "to show how cell phenotype proportions shift as the immune response progresses"; **CC BY 3.0, commercial use allowed**. Annotation CSV is provided for **Replicate 1 only** (Replicates 2 and 3 are unannotated images). — [BBBC054](https://bbbc.broadinstitute.org/BBBC054)
- **Dryad 10.5061/dryad.m0cfxpp8v**: 18 multi-TIFF **phase-contrast time-lapse** files of murine BMDMs, **5 min frame interval**, 10× (2.155 px/µm) or 20× (4.31 px/µm), labelled **M0 / M1 (100 ng/ml LPS) / M2 (20 ng/ml IL-4)** at the file level; 20 mice, 5 batches; 2.23 GB; from the paper "A data-driven approach to establishing cell motility patterns as predictors of macrophage subtypes and their relation to cell morphology" (Sun, Kesapragada). The record does not indicate per-cell annotation files. — [Dryad m0cfxpp8v](https://datadryad.org/dataset/doi:10.5061/dryad.m0cfxpp8v)
- **PhagoStat** supplies a binary genotype label (**wild-type vs FTD microglia**) over full 7 h videos with dense instance masks — a per-cell *mask* with a per-condition *label*. — [Zenodo 10803492](https://zenodo.org/records/10803492)
- **S-BIAD1871** supplies a functional-state contrast (engulfment-competent vs cytochalasin-B-blocked; bead vs phage vs control) but on fixed cells. — [BioStudies](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/biostudies/api/v1/studies/S-BIAD1871)
- Negative result: a targeted search for a public live-cell movie set with per-cell M1/M2 labels surfaced no other candidate. A bioluminescence M1/M2 gene-expression-reporter methods paper (bioRxiv 10.1101/2024.06.10.598305) images polarization at single-cell resolution in 2D and 3D but **its imaging data does not appear to be deposited**. The Dryad "Optical signature dataset for living macrophages and monocytes" (10.5061/dryad.1ns1rn8wh) has M0/M1/M2/monocyte labels but is **light-scattering signatures, not images**. — [bioRxiv 2024.06.10.598305](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2024.06.10.598305.full.pdf); [Dryad 1ns1rn8wh](https://datadryad.org/dataset/doi:10.5061/dryad.1ns1rn8wh)

### Inferences
- **Zenodo 16319732 is the unique "video + per-cell activation state" dataset** in this landscape, and its construction — a live TNF reporter channel co-imaged with a pan-macrophage channel — is the template anyone generating new data should copy, because it makes the activation label an *image channel* rather than metadata.
- Its NC-ND licence and its reliance on time-window inference for M2 are the two caveats: a model trained to predict M2 from morphology would be partly learning the authors' time-window heuristic, not an independent molecular ground truth.
- The combination **Dryad m0cfxpp8v (real movies, condition labels, 5 min, phase contrast) + Zenodo 16319732 (per-cell labels in vivo)** is the natural train/validate pairing for macrophage activation-state video classification.

### Gaps
- Whether the `Processed_Data.zip` in Zenodo 16319732 actually ships the M1/M2/M0 mode labels alongside trajectories is **not stated on the Zenodo record** (it lists trajectories, area, perimeter, distance-from-wound). The labels are definitely in the paper's structure; their presence in the deposit is **unverified**.
- No public dataset pairs immune-cell video with *omics* or with a *per-cell molecular* readout (e.g. scRNA-seq of the same imaged cells). I found none.
- BBBC054 does not state whether the three replicates share the same FOV grid, nor the number of labelled cells.

## Q7. Large general live-cell video corpora usable for pretraining (non-immune-specific), with volumes

### Takeaway
For scale, the usable pretraining pool is: **ComplexEye 186.8 GB** (immune, CC BY 4.0), **PhagoStat 94.7 GB** (immune, CC BY 4.0), **LIVECell 5,239 images / 1,686,352 cells** (static frames from time-lapse, includes BV-2 microglia), **DeepCell DynamicNuclearNet ~130 videos / ~6.7 K frames / ~600 K annotated cells** (non-commercial academic licence only), **Cell Tracking Challenge ~5 GB across 2D+Time** and **CTMC 768 MB / 86 videos / ~152 K frames** (CC BY-NC-SA 3.0). Licences, not volume, are the binding constraint: DeepCell and Cellpose are non-commercial, CTMC is NC-SA, Zenodo 16319732 is NC-ND.

### Cited Findings
- **LIVECell** (Edlund, Jackson, Khalid et al., *Nature Methods* 18:1038–1045, 2021, DOI 10.1038/s41592-021-01249-6): **5,239 annotated phase-contrast images**, **1,686,352 individual annotated cells**; **8 cell lines** — A172 (human glioblastoma), BT-474 (breast), **BV-2 (mouse microglia)**, Huh7 (hepatocellular), MCF7 (breast), SH-SY5Y (neuroblastoma), SkBr3, SK-OV-3; original acquisitions **1,408 × 1,040 px**, each cropped into four **704 × 520 px** annotated images; source imaging was **time-lapse, wells imaged every 4 h for 3–5 days**, cells from early seeding to full confluence; **the annotated images are frames sampled from those series — there is no separate time-lapse subset**. Data + models + configs at **https://sartorius-research.github.io/LIVECell/** and **doi 10.6084/m9.figshare.14931555**; code at **github.com/sartorius-research/LIVECell**. **The paper does not state a licence for the dataset itself** (the article is CC BY 4.0); a third-party directory lists MIT, which is **not authoritative**. Total data volume not stated. Pixel size is ambiguous in the paper: the stated FOV (0.875 × 0.645 mm for a 704 × 520 crop) implies ~1.24 µm/px, while the paper's size thresholds (320 µm² ≈ 500 px; 970 µm² ≈ 1,500 px) imply ~0.65 µm/px — **an internal inconsistency worth resolving before using the scale bar**. — [PMC8440198](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8440198/); [Sartorius LIVECell article page](https://www.sartorius.com/en/products/live-cell-imaging-analysis/live-cell-analysis-resources/livecell-large-scale-dataset-label-free-live-cell-segmentation-article)
- **DeepCell DynamicNuclearNet / Caliban** (Schwartz et al. 2023): training set for nuclear **segmentation and tracking**; **licensed under a modified Apache licence for non-commercial academic use only**; distributed through the `deepcell.datasets` module and requires a **DeepCell API key** (`DynamicNuclearNetTracking(version='1.0')`); tracking batches contain raw fluorescent nuclear images, segmentation masks, and lineage records (cell IDs, frames each cell appears in, parent→daughter division links). Independently described as **130 videos of fluorescently labelled nuclei from 5 cell types, ~600 K cells with segmentation and tracking annotations**; a DREEM dataset page lists **91 train / 27 val / 12 test videos, ~6.7 K frames**; the Caliban preprint says annotators tracked complete movies of **42–71 frames**. **Registration/API-key required — report under "registration-gated", not freely downloadable.** — [DeepCell DynamicNuclearNet docs](https://deepcell.readthedocs.io/en/latest/data-gallery/dynamicnuclearnet.html); [Caliban app gallery](https://deepcell.readthedocs.io/en/master/app-gallery/caliban.html); [Trackastra, arXiv 2405.15700](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2405.15700); [Caliban preprint](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/803205v3.full-text)
- **Cellpose training dataset**: `cellpose.org/dataset` is a **gated download** — requires entering an institutional email and accepting **HHMI Research Content Terms**, which permit "limited **non-commercial**, educational, research and personal use only", allow HHMI to revise terms and terminate access at any time, and require deletion of all copies on termination. The page does **not** list image counts, cell types or file sizes. **Report as registration-required and non-commercial.** — [cellpose.org/dataset](https://www.cellpose.org/dataset)
- **Cell Tracking Challenge 2D+Time**: 10 datasets, per-dataset train+test sizes summing to roughly **5.0 GB** (largest: BF-C2DL-HSC 1.6 + 1.6 GB; BF-C2DL-MuSC 1.2 + 1.3 GB; PhC-C2DL-PSC 124 + 106 MB; Fluo-N2DL-HeLa 182 + 168 MB; smallest DIC-C2DH-HeLa 37 + 41 MB). Frame intervals 5–30 min; pixel sizes 0.125–1.6 µm. **No licence stated on the page.** — [CTC 2D datasets](https://celltrackingchallenge.net/2d-datasets/)
- **CTMC-v1**: **86 videos, 14 cell lines, ~152,498 frames, 400 × 320 px, bounding-box + track-ID annotations, CC BY-NC-SA 3.0, 768 MB + 3.7 MB labels.** — [MOTChallenge CTMC-v1](https://motchallenge.net/data/CTMC-v1/)
- **ComplexEye Zenodo**: **186.8 GB, 21 ZIPs, CC BY 4.0**, raw 1280 × 800 8-bit grayscale movies at 8 s/frame, ~1 h each, >1,000 movies. This is the **largest openly licensed live-cell video corpus of a single immune cell type** that I located. — [Zenodo 7962144](https://zenodo.org/records/7962144); [PMC10713721](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10713721/)
- **PhagoStat**: **94.7 GB, CC BY 4.0**, with 2.86 M instance masks total. — [Zenodo 10803492](https://zenodo.org/records/10803492)
- Large BioImage Archive general live-cell corpora (non-immune) usable for pretraining, by file count: **S-BIAD2001** "Live-cell analyses with unsegmented images to study cancer cell response to modified T cell therapy" — **165,737 files** (contains T-cell-therapy co-culture imaging, 2025-06-04); **S-BIAD890** neuron-glia co-culture inflammatory-neurodegeneration screen — **91,856 files**; **S-BIAD2580** host-centric antiviral morphological profiling — **40,309 files**; **S-BIAD2705** 3D multiphoton liver with real/physics-based/GAN-simulated volumes — **23,149 files**; **S-BIAD547/546** *Drosophila* primary neuron microtubule imaging — 22,919 files each; **S-BIAD4064** live-cell microscopy of *S. cerevisiae* — 19,575 files; **S-BIAD499** zebrafish light-sheet Toddler-gradient mesoderm migration — 18,000 files; **S-BIAD2344** Blood Cell Painting of healthy-donor immune cells — **577,256 files** (fixed); **S-BIAD326** CLSM + DIC time-lapse image data sets — 7,857 files; **S-BIAD2515** high-content live-cell time-lapse predicting apoptosis — 6,480 files; **S-BIAD865** "Phenotypic profiling of the human genome by time-lapse microscopy reveals cell division genes (OME-NGFF)" — 538 files (the Mitocheck successor in NGFF form). — [BioStudies searches](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/biostudies/api/v1/search?query=immune%20cell%20tracking&collection=BioImages)

### Inferences
- A practical pretraining mix that is fully **CC-BY-or-freer** and immune-weighted: ComplexEye (186.8 GB brightfield neutrophils) + PhagoStat (94.7 GB microglia) + S-BIAD2196 (OME-Zarr neutrophils with tracks) + S-BIAD2133 (8 K neutrophil frames) + S-BIAD3674 (Dictyostelium chemotaxis, 30 s interval) ≈ **~290 GB of permissively licensed amoeboid-motility video**, with S-BIAD2196 and Immunemap/LTDB held out for tracking evaluation.
- Deliberately **exclude** DeepCell DynamicNuclearNet and the Cellpose dataset from any pipeline whose outputs might be commercial — both are explicitly non-commercial, and Cellpose additionally reserves the right to terminate access and require deletion.
- CTC and CTMC are too small (≈5 GB and 0.77 GB) to matter for pretraining volume; their value is purely as **standardised tracking benchmarks** with established metrics.
- **OpenCell** does not belong in a *video* catalogue: I found no evidence it contains time-lapse series, and I did not verify it either way — see Gaps.
