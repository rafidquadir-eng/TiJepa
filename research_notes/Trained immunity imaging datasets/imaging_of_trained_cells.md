# Microscopy / Video Imaging of Trained, Tolerant, and Innate-Immune-Memory Cells — and Whether Raw Image Data Were Deposited

**Scope note / headline result up front:** I could not find a single primary research paper in the trained-immunity field that deposited raw microscopy images or time-lapse videos in a dedicated image repository. A Europe PMC full-text search for `"trained immunity" AND ("BioImage Archive" OR "Image Data Resource" OR "S-BIAD" OR "idr00")` returned **hitCount = 0** — [Europe PMC REST search](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/search?query=%22trained%20immunity%22%20AND%20(%22BioImage%20Archive%22%20OR%20%22Image%20Data%20Resource%22%20OR%20%22S-BIAD%22%20OR%20%22idr00%22)&format=json&pageSize=30). Imaging in this field is published as figure panels only, with sequencing data (GEO) deposited and images held back under "available on request."

**Methodological warning for the report writer:** A generic EBI BioStudies search for "trained immunity" returns ~89,620 hits with accessions like `S-EPMC6121175` ("Epigenetics and Trained Immunity"), `S-EPMC9181299` ("LXRα Regulates oxLDL-Induced Trained Immunity in Macrophages") — [BioStudies API](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/biostudies/api/v1/search?query=trained+immunity&pageSize=50). **These `S-EPMC*` records are PubMed Central literature stubs, not BioImage Archive image deposits.** They must not be reported as deposited imaging datasets. Only `S-BIAD*` (BioImage Archive), `EMPIAR-*`, `S-BSST*` and `idr####` accessions are actual image deposits.

## Q1: Which groups have published imaging of beta-glucan- or BCG-trained monocytes/macrophages?

### Takeaway
Imaging in β-glucan/BCG training work is overwhelmingly confirmatory immunofluorescence/confocal of fixed cells or, in mouse work, intravital/multiplexed tissue imaging — not live-cell video of trained monocytes. The two most imaging-intensive examples I verified are Divangahi's β-glucan–neutrophil work (pulmonary confocal intravital microscopy) and Buffen/Netea's BCG–autophagy confocal work; neither deposited images. Notably, the canonical Chavakis β-glucan granulopoiesis paper contains **no microscopy at all**.

### Inferences
- The field's imaging is almost entirely *illustrative* rather than *primary data*: when a trained-immunity paper has a formal data-availability statement, the deposited object is sequencing (GEO) and the images are either absent from the statement or folded into "available on request."
- Radboud-centred human monocyte training work (Bekkering/Netea protocol lineage) is biochemical/epigenomic by design — cytokine ELISA, RNA-seq, ChIP — so there is little imaging to deposit. The imaging-heavy trained-immunity papers are the mouse in vivo ones (Divangahi, and to a lesser extent the nanobiologic/tumor papers), where microscopy is tissue histology and intravital rather than per-cell video.

- I found no evidence of data/resource pages on PI lab websites hosting imaging datasets; I did not reach the lab websites within my tool budget.

## Q2: Which papers image LPS tolerance / endotoxin tolerance in macrophages or monocytes with live-cell or high-content imaging?

### Takeaway
This is the one sub-area with genuine, rigorous live-cell video microscopy — two papers verified in detail (Tay/Hoffmann labs; Palaga lab). Both are single-cell signaling-dynamics studies, both are cell-line or mouse-primary rather than human, and **neither deposited raw images or movies**: one deposited extracted single-cell traces to Zenodo, the other says data are available on request.

### Cited Findings

**Wang AG et al., "Macrophage memory emerges from coordinated transcription factor and chromatin dynamics," *Cell Systems* 2025;16(2):101171, doi:10.1016/j.cels.2025.101171 (PMID 39938520 / PMC12147866)** — [Europe PMC/PMC full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12147866/)
- Lab/PI: **Savaş Tay** (lead contact; Tay Lab, Pritzker School of Molecular Engineering, University of Chicago; Chan Zuckerberg Biohub Chicago) with **Alexander Hoffmann** (UCLA) as co-supervisor — same source
- Cells/species: **primary mouse** bone-marrow-derived macrophages (BMDMs) and peritoneal macrophages (PMPs); endogenously tagged **mVenus-RelA (RelA^V/V)** on C57BL/6J; male and female mice — same source
- Replicates: live-cell NF-κB experiments at minimum biological duplicates from two independent cell preparations on different days, n > 100 cells per condition (Fig. 2); in vivo peritoneal experiments 3 mice per condition; bulk RNA-seq in triplicate; ATAC-seq replicate number not clearly stated — same source
- Stimulation protocol: six-ligand sequential screen (TNFα 10 ng/mL, IL-1β, CpG ODN 1668 100 nM, PAM2CSK4, LPS *E. coli* O111:B4 1 ng/mL, polyI:C HMW 300 ng/mL imaging / 1 µg/mL ATAC) giving **80 sequential conditions**; Ligand A for **4 h or 8 h**, then Ligand B. For bulk RNA-seq, Ligand A aspirated at 4 h and Ligand B added in fresh medium for a further 4 h (microfluidic washout per earlier published methods) — same source
- **LPS tolerance explicitly modeled**: LPS restimulation after prior LPS. In vivo: sublethal i.p. LPS endotoxemia in 6–8 week old mice (dose not stated), PMPs harvested 4 h post-injection then challenged ex vivo with TNFα or LPS; saline-injected controls — same source
- Imaging modality/settings: **Nikon Ti2 epifluorescence**, Hamamatsu ORCA-Flash4.0 V2 camera, **20× magnification** (objective NA not specified); custom **microfluidic device with 64 chambers and 14 inputs**, up to 1,000 cells per chamber, 37 °C / 5% CO₂ / 98% humidity; channels **mVenus-RelA at 508 nm, 1 s exposure** and **Hoechst at 395 nm, 50 ms exposure** for nuclear segmentation; **frame interval 6 minutes**; **total duration 12–16 h**; cell tracking >99% accuracy over up to 8-hour intervals; MATLAB 2021a processing (2023b in resources table) — same source
- Paired readouts: bulk RNA-seq (**GEO GSE260996**; STAR, featureCounts, edgeR, limma); ATAC-seq (**GEO GSE261647**; NovaSeq SP 50 bp PE; bowtie2, MACS3, JASPAR2022); no ChIP-seq generated (published ENCODE ChIP used via ChEA3); **no cytokine measurements**; multilayer perceptron on NF-κB dynamics + ATAC + motif features (8,894 samples, 5-fold CV, 72.8% accuracy over four expression classes) — same source
- **Data and code availability, verbatim excerpts:** "Raw high throughput sequencing data has been submitted to the GEO repository and is available at GSE260996" / "Single cell NFkB trace data reported in this paper is available at Zenodo: 10.5281/zenodo.14337874" / "Code is uploaded to GitHub and accessible at https://github.com/tay-lab/macrophage_memory" / "Any additional information required to reanalyze the data reported in this paper is available from the lead contact" — same source
- **Verdict: raw microscopy images/movies NOT deposited.** The Zenodo DOI **10.5281/zenodo.14337874** holds *extracted single-cell NF-κB traces*, i.e. derived quantitative data, not image stacks. The statement makes no mention of microscopy image deposition.

**Laosuk T, Kueanjinda P, Kimura H, Palaga T, "Dissection of MAPK and NF-κB p65 signaling dynamics underlying LPS tolerance in macrophages at the single-cell level," *Biotechnology Reports* 2026;51:e00966 (PMID 42325251 / PMC13279193)** — [Europe PMC full text](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC13279193/fullTextXML)
- Lab/PI: **Tanapat Palaga**, Center of Excellence in Immunology and Immune-mediated Diseases, Chulalongkorn University, Bangkok; collaborating institutions UMass Chan Medical School and Institute of Science Tokyo — same source
- Cells/species: **mouse RAW264.7 macrophage cell line** (ATCC TIB-71), stably expressing reporters — **cell line, not primary** — same source

### Inferences
- Live-cell video of endotoxin tolerance exists and is technically mature (microfluidics, 6-min frames, 12–23 h runs, KTR and endogenous-tag reporters), but it comes from **systems-biology/signaling-dynamics labs** (Tay, Hoffmann, Palaga) rather than from the trained-immunity community proper — which explains why the two literatures do not cross-cite repositories.
- The Tay lab's choice to deposit traces on Zenodo but not images is the field's best-practice ceiling at present: derived single-cell time-series are shared; the terabyte-scale image data are not.

### Gaps
- Bond et al. *Dev Cell* 2024 data availability — unretrieved (server errors).
- The precise dose of i.p. LPS in Wang et al.'s in vivo endotoxemia model was not stated in the retrievable text.
- Objective NA for the Wang et al. 20× imaging was not specified.
- No **human** monocyte endotoxin-tolerance live-imaging study was found at all.

- What exists instead is **qualitative morphology** in β-glucan training papers: β-glucan priming of monocytes produced "large cells with a morphology consistent with macrophages," and LPS- or β-glucan-treated human cells "were generally larger than control cultured macrophages" — described in the search synthesis as "qualitative observations, not Cell Painting data," drawing on [Candida albicans β-Glucan Differentiates Human Monocytes Into a Specific Subset of Macrophages, *Front Immunol* 2018 (PMC6284042)](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC6284042/) and [Continuous Exposure to Non-Soluble β-Glucans Induces Trained Immunity in M-CSF-Differentiated Macrophages (S-EPMC8208035)](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/biostudies/api/v1/search?query=trained+immunity&pageSize=50)
- The nearest methodological analogue in BioImage Archive is **S-BIAD2580, "A Host-Centric Morphological Profiling Approach to Identify Repurposed Antiviral Drugs"** (released 2026-05-26) — morphological profiling, but antiviral drug repurposing, not trained immunity — [BioImage Archive search](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/biostudies/api/v1/BioImages/search?query=macrophage+OR+monocyte+OR+neutrophil&pageSize=100)

### Inferences
- This is a genuine, well-defined white space. The reagents (β-glucan/BCG/LPS-tolerance protocols are standardized, e.g. the Bekkering 2016 human monocyte model) and the imaging methods (Cell Painting) both exist and have simply not been combined.
- Because nobody has done image-based profiling of trained cells, there is correspondingly nothing for anyone to have deposited — which is the parsimonious explanation for the zero-hit BioImage Archive/IDR result rather than a deposit-culture failure alone.

### Gaps
- I cannot rule out a very recent preprint (late 2026) doing exactly this; my searches surfaced bioRxiv records (Chatzis 2024, Borbora 2025, Le 2025) but no Cell Painting one.
- I did not search the Broad Institute's Cell Painting Gallery / cellpainting-gallery S3 index directly, which is where such a dataset would live if it existed.
